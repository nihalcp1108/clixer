About Clixer 304

We design, engineer and manufacture premium stainless steel drainage systems with a luxury industrial finish and high-performance reliability.

Company Story

Clixer 304 began with a vision to elevate bathroom drainage through precision workmanship and sustainable stainless steel solutions. Today, we serve architects, developers and high-end residential projects globally.

Mission

To create premium drainage solutions that blend elegant aesthetics with unmatched durability and functional performance.

Vision

To be the trusted partner for modern bathrooms worldwide, delivering intelligent SS304 systems that stand the test of time.

Quality Commitment

Every Clixer product is crafted from SS304 stainless steel, inspected to rigorous standards, and engineered for resistance against corrosion, heavy traffic, and everyday wear.
